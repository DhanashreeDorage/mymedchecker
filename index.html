How it works

  1. Add your medicines

    Type them in or pick from a list. Include vitamins, supplements and anything you buy without a prescription.

  2. Get a clear check

    See which combinations need attention and why, explained without medical jargon.

  3. Talk to your doctor

    Print or share your results and bring them to your next appointment or pharmacy visit.

For you, and for the people you look after

For yourself

Keep one up-to-date list of everything you take, and check it again whenever a new prescription is added.

For a parent or family member

Keep a separate list for each person you help care for, so nothing gets missed between doctors.

  • Combined effects of your whole list, such as added-up drowsiness or bleeding risk
  • Drug-to-drug interactions
  • Duplicate medicines doing the same job
  • Medicines that carry extra risk after 65
  • Supplement and over-the-counter conflicts
  • Inactive ingredients and common allergens
  • Updates when a medicine is added or changed
